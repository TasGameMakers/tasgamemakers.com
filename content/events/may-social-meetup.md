We're trying a different, slightly warmer venue this month — join us at In The Hanging Gardens, 112 Murray Street, Hobart at 5:30pm on Thursday May 28th. The venue is open air and pet friendly, so come prepared for cooler weather.

As usual, come along for chats, drinks and food. Feel free to bring a demo to showcase. All welcome — whether you're making games, playing games, or just curious about games. You don't need to be a member of Tas Game Makers to attend.
