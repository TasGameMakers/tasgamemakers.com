This month's southern Tas Game Makers meetup will be at Bevs 🍺 on Thursday 25th June, 5:30pm. Located at 189 Elizabeth St, Hobart TAS 7000 (the old Simple Cider).

They don't offer bookings, but have assured us there should be plenty of space.

A few things to note:

- There is no food available at the venue, but BYO is welcome (e.g. grab something nearby).
- The venue is pet friendly!
- This is an indoor venue, so it should be warmer.

Come along for chats, drinks and good company. Feel free to bring a demo to showcase. All welcome — whether you're making games, playing games, or just curious about games. You don't need to be a member of Tas Game Makers to attend.

## 🧑‍💻🎸 TasJam 2026 Winter Edition

We are planning a mid-year game jam to start on the night of the meetup. Similar to last year's jam, it kicks off at the meetup and finishes on the date of the next one — covering two weeks.

Details and registration to be announced before the meetup.
