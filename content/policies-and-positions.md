Tas Game Makers is a community organisation and an incorporated association. This page brings together the **policies** that govern how we operate, and the **positions** we take publicly on issues affecting people who make games in lutruwita/Tasmania.

## Policies

- **[Code of Conduct](/policies-and-positions/code-of-conduct/)** — the standards of behaviour we expect in all TasGM spaces, online and in person, so our community stays welcoming and harassment-free.
- **[Privacy Policy](/policies-and-positions/privacy/)** — how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

## Positions

- **[National Cultural Policy Submission (2026)](/policies-and-positions/ncp-2026/)** — our submission to Australia's next National Cultural Policy, making the case to fund video games as culture, and to back regional Australia, where that investment goes furthest.
